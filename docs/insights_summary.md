# Call Centre Analysis — Insights Summary

**Dataset:** 5,000 Calls
**Period:** January 2021 – March 2021
**Agents:** 8
**Support Topics:** 5

---

# Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates call centre performance across customer service operations during Q1 2021. The dashboard focuses on operational efficiency, agent effectiveness, customer satisfaction, and issue resolution outcomes.

Overall, the call centre demonstrated strong performance with an 81.1% answer rate and an 89.9% First Call Resolution (FCR) rate. Customer satisfaction remained relatively stable across agents and support topics, averaging 3.40 out of 5.

The analysis identified opportunities to reduce unanswered calls, improve technical support resolution rates, and increase overall customer satisfaction through targeted coaching and operational improvements.

---

# 1. Call Volume & Service Performance

## Key Metrics

| Metric                     | Value        |
| -------------------------- | ------------ |
| Total Calls                | 5,000        |
| Answered Calls             | 4,054        |
| Unanswered Calls           | 946          |
| Answer Rate                | 81.1%        |
| First Call Resolution Rate | 89.9%        |
| Average Speed of Answer    | 67.5 Seconds |

## Observations

* The call centre handled 5,000 customer interactions during Q1 2021.
* More than four out of five calls were successfully answered.
* Nearly one in five calls remained unanswered, representing a potential service gap.
* The overall First Call Resolution rate of 89.9% indicates strong issue resolution performance among answered calls.
* Average response time remained below 70 seconds across the organisation.

## Business Impact

Unanswered calls may negatively affect customer satisfaction and increase repeat contact volume. Reducing the unanswered call rate could improve customer experience while lowering operational workload.

# Conclusion

The call centre demonstrated strong operational performance during Q1 2021, achieving an 81.1% answer rate and an 89.9% First Call Resolution rate across 5,000 customer interactions.
